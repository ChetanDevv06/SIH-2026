# CareGrid Roadmap

This roadmap represents project direction rather than guaranteed delivery dates.

## Current Focus

### Healthcare Coordination

- Cross-tier referrals.
- Appointment scheduling.
- Doctor approval.
- Safe concurrent booking.
- Referral response timeout/escalation.
- Patient/local-record workflows.
- Consent-aware data handling.

### Facility Availability

- Human-verified beds.
- Doctor availability.
- Equipment availability.
- Medicine inventory.
- Last-updated timestamps.
- Stale-data warnings and escalation.

### Offline First

- Raspberry Pi/local facility edge operation.
- Synchronization after connectivity recovery.
- Explicit offline and stale states.
- Synchronization conflict handling.

### Ambulance

- Ambulance-side facility discovery.
- Bed/doctor/equipment queries.
- Raspberry Pi integration.
- LoRa experimentation and field validation.

### Constrained Networks

- Keypad/USSD workflows.
- SMS notification/fallback mechanisms.
- Low-bandwidth operational workflows.

## Future / Validation

- Field validation of connectivity assumptions.
- Hardware deployment validation.
- LoRa range/reliability validation.
- USSD provider/shortcode integration.
- ABDM/ABHA integration.
- Operational analytics.
- Production deployment hardening.

## Prioritization

Prioritize patient safety, data integrity, offline reliability, security/privacy, usability, low-bandwidth operation, and maintainability.
